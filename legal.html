Privacy Policy

Ember Chime · Free mobile application

Effective as of 2026-05-18
Overview

This privacy policy applies to the Ember Chime app (referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, provided as a free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

🔒 Our commitment: Ember Chime does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything runs entirely on your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.
Information Collection and Use

The Application itself does not collect, store, or process any personal information. Specifically:

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No location data collected or transmitted
  • No AI processing of your data
  • All settings (interval, sound, spiritual profile, location coordinates) are stored exclusively on your device using Android's local SharedPreferences and are never transmitted anywhere
📍 Location: If you use the automatic location detection feature, your coordinates are used solely to calculate local sunrise and sunset times. This calculation happens entirely on-device and your location is never transmitted to any server.
🤖 AI: The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence technologies to process your data or provide features.
Third Party Services

The Application is distributed through Google Play Store. Google Play Services may collect certain technical and device information independently, according to their own Privacy Policy. The Application itself has no control over this data collection.

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

Beyond Google Play Services, the Application does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or data brokers.

Opt-Out

Since the Application does not collect any personal data, there is nothing to opt out of. You may uninstall the Application at any time using standard processes available on your device or via Google Play Store. All locally stored settings will be removed with the Application.

Data Retention

The Application stores your preferences (interval settings, chosen sounds, spiritual profile, and optionally your location coordinates for sunrise/sunset calculation) locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the Application.

If you have any concerns, contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz.

Children

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If a child under 13 has provided personal information, it will be immediately deleted from our servers.

If you are a parent or guardian and aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information and provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect information processed and maintained.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes by updating this page. Continued use of the Application is deemed approval of all changes.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you acknowledge that Ember Chime operates entirely on your device without collecting or transmitting personal data, and that Google Play Services may independently collect certain technical information as described in their own Privacy Policy.
